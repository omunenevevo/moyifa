Who We Are

Masturbation Overcomers Youth Initiative (MOYI) is a transformative charitable organization founded and led by Elijah Kitondekiggya, dedicated to empowering young people to overcome habits and behaviors that impede their personal, mental, emotional, and spiritual growth. Our organization is built on the belief that every young person has the potential to live a life of discipline, purpose, and fulfillment, and that with the right guidance and support, they can rise above challenges that threaten their future. At MOYI, our mission is to provide holistic support and mentorship to youth, equipping them with the knowledge, tools, and emotional resilience needed to break free from destructive patterns. We focus not only on awareness but also on practical strategies for self-control, mental well-being, and personal growth, ensuring that young people are empowered to take charge of their lives in a positive and meaningful way. Our programs are designed to address the challenges faced by youth in today’s rapidly changing world. Through educational workshops, mentorship sessions, counseling programs, and community engagement initiatives, MOYI creates safe and supportive environments where young people can openly discuss their struggles, learn coping strategies, and build lifelong skills for personal development. We emphasize the importance of self-awareness, self-discipline, and emotional intelligence, recognizing that these qualities are fundamental to achieving success and fulfillment. Beyond individual transformation, MOYI is committed to building a strong, resilient community of young leaders who inspire and support one another. By fostering peer-to-peer mentorship, networking opportunities, and leadership development programs, we aim to cultivate a generation of confident, responsible, and purpose-driven youth who can positively impact their families, communities, and society at large. MOYI also prioritizes collaboration with families, educators, and community leaders, understanding that a collective approach amplifies the impact of our work. We are committed to raising awareness about the challenges facing youth and advocating for supportive structures that nurture healthy habits, positive mindsets, and holistic well-being. Under the visionary leadership of Elijah Kitondekiggya, MOYI has grown into more than a charity — it is a movement dedicated to breaking cycles of destructive behavior, empowering youth, and promoting lasting transformation. Your support enables us to expand our reach, provide critical resources, and implement innovative programs that touch lives and transform futures. Every contribution, no matter the size, directly supports our initiatives, helping young people build resilience, confidence, and purpose. By joining MOYI, you become part of a mission to empower youth, transform lives, and create a brighter, more disciplined, and focused generation. Together, we can inspire change, nurture potential, and guide young people toward lives of freedom, purpose, and success. Support MOYI today and be a catalyst for transformation..

Our Mission

To educate, support, mentor, and restore youths struggling with masturbation and related sexual addictions through awareness, counseling, accountability, faith-based and life-skills programs.

Our Vision

To see a generation of youths free from sexual addiction, walking in self-discipline, confidence, spiritual strength, and purpose.

Our Core Values

Awareness & Education

Teach youths the psychological, physical, spiritual, and social effects of sexual addiction.

Support & Accountability

Create safe spaces (groups, mentorship, forums) where youths can speak freely and grow together.

Rehabilitation & Counseling

Provide guidance, mentorship, and referrals for professional or faith-based counseling..

Character & Discipline Building

Equip youths with habits, routines, and life skills that strengthen self-control.
